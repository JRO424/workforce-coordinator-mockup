Who owns this today?

In most growing companies, the honest answer is nobody. The work is split across people who are already doing another job.

Founder

Owns escalations, exceptions, urgent decisions, and the anxiety of not knowing what is exposed.

Operations

Chases vendors, managers, documents, deadlines, and systems without a dedicated HR function.

Managers

Handle employee issues differently because the process lives in memory instead of a standard.

Payroll vendor

Processes inputs, but does not own the full people system around those inputs.

HR software

Stores pieces of the work, but does not make sure the work is finished.

Documents

Exist somewhere, but nobody owns whether they are current, complete, and defensible.
